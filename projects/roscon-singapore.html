02 — DEEP DIVE

ROSCon Singapore

ROS2 Conference Community Networking

The Experience

Attended ROSCon Singapore — the premier global conference for the ROS community. Engaged with maintainers, researchers, and engineers building the next generation of robotics middleware, tooling, and applications across industries.

Key Takeaways

  • Deep dives into ROS2 middleware improvements — DDS alternatives, performance tuning, and real-time guarantees
  • The evolving landscape of ros2_control and hardware abstraction for manipulation
  • Conversations with teams deploying ROS2 at scale in agriculture, logistics, and household robotics
  • Emerging patterns for integrating learning-based policies into ROS2 pipelines
  • Networking with the global robotics community — connecting with engineers solving similar problems across different domains

Why It Matters

Conferences like ROSCon are where the gap between what's possible in research and what's practical in production gets narrowed. The insights from Singapore directly influenced architectural decisions in our manipulation stack — from how we structure our control loops to how we think about middleware performance.
